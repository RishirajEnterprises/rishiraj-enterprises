About the studio

A regional practice, built on national-grade standards

Rishiraj Enterprises is a multidisciplinary architecture, interior design and construction firm based in Guwahati. For over fifteen years we've worked alongside developers, hospitality groups and public institutions to deliver buildings that hold up to daily use and still feel considered years later.

Every project is led by the same core team from concept through handover — one point of contact, one set of drawings, one standard of finish — so nothing gets lost in translation between design and site.

"Good architecture in this region has to answer to the climate, the site and the budget — in that order. We design for all three." — Founding Principal, Rishiraj Enterprises
30+ Years designing and building across Assam and the Northeast
How we work

From first meeting to final key handover

01

Consultation

We visit the site, understand your brief, budget and timeline, and confirm feasibility before any design work begins.

02

Design & Planning

Concept drawings, 3D visualisation, material selection and statutory approvals, refined with you until every detail is signed off.

03

Execution

In-house project managers and vetted contractors keep construction on schedule, with weekly site reports and site visits.

04

Handover & Support

A final walkthrough, snag-list closure, and a maintenance point of contact for the first year after handover.

What we do

Services

Comprehensive solutions from blueprint to reality — engaged individually or as a single turnkey scope.

Architecture

Concept design, structural planning and statutory drawings for commercial, hospitality and residential buildings.

Interior Design

Tailored, liveable environments that balance ergonomics with durable, high-end material finishes.

Project Management

Dedicated site supervision, vendor coordination and schedule tracking so timelines are met without compromising quality.

Turnkey Construction

Single-contract build execution — civil, MEP and finishing — delivered to the approved design and specification.

Renovation & Retrofit

Structural upgrades and full interior refits for ageing properties, hotels and office spaces, with minimal downtime.

Sustainability Consulting

Passive cooling, daylighting and material choices that lower long-term operating and maintenance costs.
